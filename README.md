# He Zhang (张贺) — Academic Homepage

This repository hosts the official academic homepage of **He Zhang**
(**Zhang He**, **Zhanghe**, 张贺):

<https://zhhe11.github.io/>

He Zhang is a Ph.D. candidate in Artificial Intelligence at the Hong Kong
University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), **HKUST(GZ)**, and received
his B.E. in Automation from **Tsinghua University**.

His research focuses on reinforcement learning, robot learning, multimodal
imitation learning, world models, continual learning, and embodied artificial
intelligence.
